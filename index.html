The Cakehouse

Every cake here starts as a conversation — a date, a venue, a feeling you can’t quite put into words, and ends as the thing everyone gathers around.

Eckle’s is a home bakery run by Jesse, and every cake is designed around one occasion: yours. Wedding tiers, vintage Lambeth hearts, birthday cakes that look exactly like the thing they love.

Every cake is filled with American buttercream and finished in white chocolate ganache or, if you’d prefer, entirely vegan buttercream.

As special as the day itself.

“Your wedding cake should feel just as special as the day itself.”

From elegant tiered cakes to statement designs and dessert displays (cascading florals, intricate Lambeth piping or something entirely contemporary), each wedding cake is created to complement your celebration, and to taste as good as it looks.

Tiers
Single tiers from £100. Two and three tiers from £250. Four or more, quoted to order.
Finish
American buttercream and white chocolate ganache, or fully vegan buttercream.
On the day
Delivered and set up at your venue, from £15 depending on location.
VIP
For two tiers or more: a private taster and consultation, favours, stand and knife hire, a preservation kit, photography props and a follow-up.
